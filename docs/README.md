## Reading the numbers

Three things are worth knowing before quoting any figure from this project:

1. **Provenance differs per agent.** Battery, both quality classifiers and carbon rest on real
   public data. Supply Chain Risk is synthetic. Compound Risk's truck→supplier linkage is a fixed
   illustrative table. The dashboard labels each one.
2. **Several headline numbers changed during the rebuild**, because the originals were measured
   against contaminated splits, broken labels, or a buggy baseline. See
   [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md) for the before/after and the ADRs for the reasoning.
3. **Where an evaluation is weak, it says so** rather than being omitted — 73.1% of battery rows
   are right-censored and excluded, and Fleet Readiness has no expert baseline at all.
